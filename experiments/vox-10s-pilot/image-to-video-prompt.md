# 10s Image-to-Video Prompt — VISUALS ONLY

> This prompt is for an image-to-video model after the approved style frame / start composite exists.

## Input

Use the approved start frame containing:
- real Dad photo cutout;
- warm paper background;
- accurate Australia SVG;
- Adelaide marker;
- editable text already composited outside the model.

## Prompt

```
Animate this existing editorial paper-collage frame for exactly ten seconds.

Keep the composition, Dad photo identity, paper materials, map geometry and palette locked to the supplied image.

Motion sequence:

First, hold the real Dad photo cutout as the visual anchor while the camera makes a very slow, controlled push in. Paper elements move with a subtle handmade 12fps stutter inside a 24fps timeline.

Then the Adelaide marker receives one restrained yellow highlight pulse. Do not change the map shape or labels.

Near the final third of the clip, a handcrafted paper-cut Australian kangaroo slides in from the right in four to six stepped stop-motion poses and settles at the right edge. The kangaroo is playful and calm. No punch, no fighting, no impact and no aggressive attack.

Dad remains stable and recognizable throughout. Do not animate Dad’s facial features. Do not change his glasses, face shape, clothing or expression.

Keep all text unchanged and readable. Do not generate new words.

Visual language: premium editorial documentary, muted cream charcoal and gray palette, one strong highlighter-yellow accent, tactile paper texture, photo cutout drop shadows, subtle halftone print texture, restrained handmade imperfection, calm pacing, one visual idea, no glossy CGI, no corporate explainer look.

No United States imagery, no USA flag, no America map, no American landmarks, no unrelated B-roll.

VISUALS ONLY.
No narration.
No dialogue.
No generated captions.
No generated music.
No generated sound effects.
```

## Why visuals-only

The project already had narration drift.

For this pilot, narration is placed separately at exact Remotion frames.

The AI video model is not allowed to control the voice timing.
