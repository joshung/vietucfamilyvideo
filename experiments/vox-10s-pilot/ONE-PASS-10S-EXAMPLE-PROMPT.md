## Review

Only judge three things:

1. Does the frame look like premium editorial design rather than AI slop?
2. Is Dad unmistakably the real subject and clearly Australian/Adelaide?
3. Does the kangaroo payoff feel timed and restrained rather than chaotic?

If any answer is no, do not continue to 120 seconds.
