# 13 — Asset-to-Shot Map v01

## Purpose

This document connects the factual v02 shot list to the supplied archive catalog without pretending that every catalog image is historical footage from the year being narrated.

## Global rule

For historical beats **Adelaide / 1996 / 1997 / 2006**:

- use a real period photo only when its date/context is verified;
- otherwise show Dad/people as neutral archive portraits plus map/timeline/typography;
- do not fake documentary evidence with photoreal AI.

## Visual QC before selecting any URL

For every candidate:
1. fetch original image;
2. verify it decodes at sufficient resolution;
3. inspect faces/identity;
4. confirm no misleading chronology;
5. confirm consent/project-rights assumptions;
6. crop-test 16:9 and 9:16;
7. register selected asset ID in the shot metadata.
