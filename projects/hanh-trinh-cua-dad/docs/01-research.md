## Research insertion points

The current script deliberately contains places where verified facts can later be inserted without rebuilding the whole film:

1. Australia opening card — hometown/year.
2. Map departure beat — year/reason for first trip.
3. Vietnam montage — first city/location.
4. Emotional turn — one real anecdote or quote.
5. Ending — what Dad/VietUcFamily is doing today.

## Fact-check standard before final voice-over

Each real-world biographical claim should have:

```
claim:
source:
source_type: first_party | document | interview | public_record | secondary
confidence: high | medium | low
approved_for_narration: true | false
```

## Creative recommendation

Keep the first version fact-light and emotion-heavy until Dad's biography page or a direct interview is available. A confident film with five verified facts is better than a detailed film with twenty invented ones.
