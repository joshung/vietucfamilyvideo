# 00 — Creative Brief

## Objective

Introduce Dad through a memorable two-minute journey from Australia to Vietnam, establishing the emotional idea behind VietUcFamily without turning the film into a dry biography.

## Audience

Primary:
- Vietnamese and Australian family/community viewers;
- people discovering VietUcFamily for the first time.

Secondary:
- younger viewers who respond to fast visual storytelling, humor, maps, photo cutouts and kinetic text.

## Audience outcome

At the end, the viewer should think:

> "I came for the kangaroo joke; I stayed because this is actually a warm story about finding home in another country."

## Tone

- warm;
- clever;
- affectionate;
- deadpan humor;
- never mocking Dad;
- never treating Vietnamese culture as an exotic punchline.

## Editorial style

Use the grammar of a modern editorial explainer / visual essay:
- archival-photo cutouts;
- animated maps;
- bold labels;
- kinetic typography;
- simple diagrams;
- paper/textural collage;
- rapid but legible editing;
- deadpan narrator;
- sound-design punchlines.

Do not copy another publisher's logo, type system, exact graphic package, music identity, or branded lower thirds.

## Core narrative

**Setup:** Dad is from Australia.  
**Comic myth:** Naturally, this means he has apparently been preparing for international diplomacy by boxing kangaroos.  
**Journey:** Vietnam enters the map.  
**Comic objective:** He imagines "conquering" the local boss: the buffalo.  
**Reversal:** Instead of Dad conquering Vietnam, Vietnam conquers Dad.  
**Payoff:** What starts as a journey becomes affection, belonging, and the story behind VietUcFamily.

## Hard constraints

- Runtime: about 120 seconds.
- Dad's real face must remain recognizable in documentary/present-day moments.
- Kangaroo/buffalo sequences must read as fantasy/comedy, not documentary fact.
- Do not invent exact biographical facts.
- Website should appear near the ending as the current destination/continuation of the story.
- AI generation should be modular, generally ≤10 seconds per generated clip unless current model support says otherwise.
