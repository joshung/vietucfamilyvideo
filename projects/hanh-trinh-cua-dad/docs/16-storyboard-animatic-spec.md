## Animatic approval tests

The animatic passes only if:
- hook works without sound;
- first factual idea appears by 0:24;
- years are legible;
- “8” lands emotionally;
- recent archive is not mistaken for early archive;
- Dad remains the visual anchor;
- ending is readable for at least ~2 seconds;
- total runtime remains within 118–122 seconds.
