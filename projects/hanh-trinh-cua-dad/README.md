## Creative promise

A warm, funny, fast-moving introduction to Dad's journey from Australia to Vietnam. Humor comes from obviously exaggerated fantasy gags — fighting a kangaroo and preparing to "conquer" a buffalo — while the emotional spine stays respectful: Dad thought he was going somewhere new; Vietnam became somewhere he loved.

## Important truth rule

The kangaroo fight and buffalo-conquest beats are **deliberate comedic fiction** and must be visually signaled as exaggeration. They are not biographical claims.

The current environment could not retrieve the supplied biography page at:

http://www.vietucfamily.org/gioi_thieu

Therefore no exact dates, hometowns, jobs, relationship chronology, or other personal facts are invented in this draft. Those details must be verified before being added.

## Dad likeness rule

Documentary/present-day shots should use approved real photographs or footage of Dad.

For AI-assisted shots:
- use approved Dad reference images;
- preserve facial identity as a QC target, not a guarantee;
- prefer subtle image-to-video motion for real-photo moments;
- keep fantasy gags clearly stylized/collage-like so the audience understands they are jokes;
- do not let an AI-generated face replace the authoritative real Dad reference.
